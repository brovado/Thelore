Getting Started

A quick guide for returning and new players.

Returning players

Read the Chapter 1 recap and visit Our Characters. Your two Chapter 1 faction endorsements and outstanding story threads will be filled in as the original records are checked.

New players

Choose a hometown and one faction affiliation to get started. You can develop the rest of your history later. See the character entry guide.
