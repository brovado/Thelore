Factions & Coalescent Society

Public-facing summaries of the groups shaping Saint Lore. No hidden agendas here.

Flame Seekers

A militant-adjacent expeditionary guild focused on direct action, retrieval, and frontier security.

Arcane Consortium

Scholars and engineers who regulate magical infrastructure and run research initiatives.

Radiant Order

Faith-led guardians who emphasize protection, ceremony, and steady moral guidance.

Shadow Syndicate

Information brokers and private operators who manage undercity logistics and soft influence.

Verdant Enclave

Stewards of natural balance who monitor the wild edges of the Western Line.

Embered Circle

Artisans, mystics, and survivalists who value resilience and communal memory.

Coalescent Society

A neutral global body with a small local lodge in Saint Lore. They coordinate cross-faction diplomacy and maintain the shared transit protocols.
