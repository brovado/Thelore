Factions of Thelore

A public reference for adventurers.

The city's factions shaped the party's journey toward the Coalescence Society. Each returning adventurer earned two faction endorsements through their Chapter One missions.

The encyclopedia contains searchable faction profiles and connections to their known buildings and members.
