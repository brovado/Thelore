Saint Lore (Thel’aranor)

The western capital built atop ancient infrastructure, where modern life rests on dormant power.

City Overview

Saint Lore is a layered city of old stone and new steel. The streets bustle with guild traffic, inter-faction diplomacy, and an ever-present awareness of the Western Line that runs beneath the city.

The city’s ancient name, Thel’aranor, still appears on foundational signage and sealed gates. Modern Saint Lore governance is practical and civic, built around keeping the lines open and the city stable.

Thel’aranor Gate (Saint Lore Hub)

The primary station is known as Thel’aranor Gate (Saint Lore Hub). It is a mix of ancient infrastructure and modern upgrades: the civic rail authority bolted signage and safety railings onto a cavernous chamber that predates the city itself.

For players, the Gate is a staging room. It is safe to discuss, inspect, and select a destination before committing to travel.
