Build your next adventurer

For new, replacement, and rebuilt characters alike. You do not need to return as a previous character.

Character checklist

  1. Start at level 4. Complete your character sheet through level 4.
  2. Use standard array: 15 / 14 / 13 / 12 / 10 / 8. Assign one score to each ability, then apply the ability score increases from your background under the 2024 rules.
  3. Choose any D&D 2024 / 5.5 class, subclass, and species. In the 2024 rules, classes gain their subclass at class level 3, so a character with four levels in one class already has a subclass. If you split levels between classes, follow each class's own level progression.
  4. Select your importing faction. This is the faction bringing or recommending your character into the expedition. Browse the Faction Registry for its public role and connections.
  5. Choose your hometown. Explore the World Compendium and world map for inspiration.
  6. Bring your sheet and character name. Include your background and the features, spells, and equipment choices needed for play.

Those are the campaign build instructions. Further backstory can grow during play; no specific returning character is assumed.
