Joining the Expedition

Start small. Grow during play.

New and replacement characters need only two pieces of world history to begin: one faction affiliation and a hometown. Bring your character's name and the normal character sheet details required for play.

You can develop your character's relationships, ambitions, backstory, and second faction connection later. Existing characters earned two faction endorsements through Chapter 1 missions as part of joining the Coalescence Society; new arrivals are not required to recreate those adventures before joining.

Questions to consider (optional)

Why did your faction recommend you? What made you leave home? Who might recognize you when you arrive?

Talk with the DM to confirm a faction and hometown that fit the setting.
