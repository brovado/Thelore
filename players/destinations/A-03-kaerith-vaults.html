A-03 — Kaerith Vaults

4–6 sessions Restricted

What players know on arrival

  • Old defensive arrays disrupt signals and equipment.
  • Entry protocols are tighter than most Western Line sites.
  • Structural depth suggests long-term exploration.

Why go here?

  • High-stakes arc for teams seeking complex challenges.
  • Potential access to sealed archives or infrastructure.
  • Extended missions with layered objectives.

Soft lock warning

Commitment starts once you exit transit at the Kaerith Vaults. Expect a longer deployment.

Return state note

Return depends on securing safe routes and stabilizing local systems. Expect staggered extraction timing.
