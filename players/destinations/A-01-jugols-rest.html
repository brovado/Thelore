A-01 — Jugol’s Rest

1 session Active

What players know on arrival

  • A compact desert outpost with crystalline walls that hum in the wind.
  • Simple, direct terrain suited to quick recon and retrieval.
  • Local signage references short-term deployments and safe return windows.

Why go here?

  • Fast-paced objective with minimal downtime.
  • Clear lines of approach for scouting and recovery.
  • Good opener for teams looking to test the Western Line.

Soft lock warning

Commitment starts once you exit transit at Jugol’s Rest. Choose only if you are ready for a full session on-site.

Return state note

Return is typically available after the local objective resolves. Expect a brief debrief at Thel’aranor Gate.
