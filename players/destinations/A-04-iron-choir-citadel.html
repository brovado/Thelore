A-04 — Iron Choir Citadel

2–3 sessions Unsurveyed

What players know on arrival

  • Fortified architecture and resonance-driven halls.
  • Transit data suggests dormant defense systems.
  • Limited ground reports beyond the outer corridors.

Why go here?

  • Tactical exploration with clear structural landmarks.
  • Potential alliances or salvage of legacy tech.
  • Short arc with high atmosphere and concentrated encounters.

Soft lock warning

Commitment starts once you exit transit at the Iron Choir Citadel. Plan for multiple sessions.

Return state note

Return access depends on clearing the primary approach route. Extraction may require a secured exit point.
