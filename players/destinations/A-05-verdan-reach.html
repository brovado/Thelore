A-05 — Verdan Reach

4–6 sessions Active

What players know on arrival

  • Temperate canopy and resurgent green growth.
  • Geothermal readings fluctuate along the line.
  • Known to support longer deployments.

Why go here?

  • Extended arc with layered travel and survival beats.
  • Opportunities for exploration, diplomacy, or rescue.
  • Potential to shift Western Line access long-term.

Soft lock warning

Commitment starts once you exit transit at Verdan Reach. Expect a longer campaign arc.

Return state note

Return timing depends on stabilizing local conditions and securing the return approach.
