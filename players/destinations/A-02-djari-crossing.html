A-02 — Djari Crossing

2–3 sessions Unsurveyed

What players know on arrival

  • Dense growth and humid air surround the crossing points.
  • Surface vents hint at an overgrown approach route.
  • Signals are intermittent and local maps are incomplete.

Why go here?

  • Exploration-heavy arc with room for scouting and discovery.
  • Ideal for groups who like environmental challenges.
  • Potential to open new lines of travel or trade.

Soft lock warning

Commitment starts once you exit transit at Djari Crossing. Plan for a multi-session stay.

Return state note

Return windows depend on local stabilization and transit readiness. Expect a staged extraction.
