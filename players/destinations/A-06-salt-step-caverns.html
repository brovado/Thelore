A-06 — Salt-Step Caverns

2–3 sessions Unsurveyed

What players know on arrival

  • Layered caverns with mineral-rich air and shifting terrain.
  • Geothermal pulses have been detected along the route.
  • Expect a mix of subterranean navigation and surface access points.

Why go here?

  • Exploration and traversal challenges in a unique environment.
  • Opportunity to secure a stable passage for future travel.
  • Good fit for teams who like navigation and hazard management.

Soft lock warning

Commitment starts once you exit transit at the Salt-Step Caverns. Plan for a multi-session deployment.

Return state note

Return access depends on finding a safe route through the caverns. Expect a structured exit plan.
