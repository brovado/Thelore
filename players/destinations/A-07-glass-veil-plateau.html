A-07 — Glass Veil Plateau

4–6 sessions Restricted

What players know on arrival

  • High plateau with thin air and shifting light phenomena.
  • Reports mention mirage-like distortions across the horizon.
  • Entry protocols are restricted and monitored.

Why go here?

  • Long-form arc with environmental hazards and discovery.
  • Potential to secure rare resources or knowledge.
  • Ideal for teams ready for extended, high-altitude missions.

Soft lock warning

Commitment starts once you exit transit at the Glass Veil Plateau. Prepare for a longer arc.

Return state note

Return depends on reestablishing safe routes through the plateau. Expect staggered extraction.
