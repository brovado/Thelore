A-08 — Storm-Edge Terminus

4–6 sessions Unsurveyed

What players know on arrival

  • Westernmost terminus with volatile weather and strong tides.
  • Signal locks are recent and still stabilizing.
  • Expect wide-open terrain and unpredictable conditions.

Why go here?

  • Large-scale expedition for groups seeking high stakes.
  • Potential to secure the farthest Western Line access point.
  • Strong fit for extended arcs with multiple objectives.

Soft lock warning

Commitment starts once you exit transit at Storm-Edge Terminus. Plan for a sustained deployment.

Return state note

Return access depends on stabilizing the terminus and securing a safe departure window.
