Thel’aranor Gate Transit Hub

The Transit Room is the safe staging chamber beneath Saint Lore where the Western Line is chosen.

Transit Room Concept

Inside the Transit Room you can review destination briefings, compare durations, and decide your next arc. It is a planning space — not a commitment.

Duration Tiers

  • 1 session Quick deployments or reconnaissance.
  • 2–3 sessions Standard missions with layered objectives.
  • 4–6 sessions Extended commitments with complex return conditions.

Soft Lock Rule

Commitment occurs after exiting transit at a destination. You can change your mind while still in the Transit Room.
