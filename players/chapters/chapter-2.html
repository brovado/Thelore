Chapter II — Echoes Along the Western Line

The active arc begins at Thel’aranor Gate (Saint Lore Hub) and moves outward along the Western Line.

Opening Dossier

The city has stabilized just enough to reopen the Western Line. The Transit Room is now the staging floor for choosing a mission, with each destination offering a different tone, challenge, and duration.

How Chapter 2 Works

  • Modular structure: each destination is a contained arc with its own stakes.
  • Player-driven: choose a destination to set the story’s direction.
  • Soft lock-in: commitment begins only after you exit transit at the destination.
