Chapter 2 — The Golden Line

The world is opening, but the journey has not yet begun.

What the party knows

The Forge's awakening revealed an ancient transit system and a destination board bearing unfamiliar names and symbols. The board is not yet an operational travel menu. There is much to learn before anyone can safely venture along the lost line.

A new responsibility

The Coalescence Society is taking responsibility for operating and investigating the railroad. Its local leadership has limited help and may turn to proven adventurers as the work begins.

What comes next?

Investigation, faction relationships, and opportunities to explore beyond familiar territory. Routes and destinations will be revealed through play; older Western Line listings are retired prototypes and not current campaign options.
