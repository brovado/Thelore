The City and the Deep

In Jugol's Rest, the companions became entangled with the city's factions, the mysterious Underveins beneath its streets, and the remains of an older world. Their journeys were also a path toward the Coalescence Society: each aspiring member sought endorsements from two factions by undertaking missions and building trust.

Companions and Changes

Rufus, Beeran, Mimun, Eli, and Thad remain part of the returning company. Gruxx stepped away to work at the Verdant Grove with its sage; Adrian Sharpe subsequently joined the adventurers.

The Accidental Awakening

While following an unrelated map, the party accidentally restored an ancient Forge. It became operational, though imperfect, and powered a connected transit terminal. A destination board lit up with unknown names and sigils, but it remained read-only. An unexplained avatar manifestation accompanied the awakening.

What Remains

The Coalescence Society raised a protective barrier around the Forge. The adventurers are regarded as local heroes, but the Forge's wider purpose remains uncertain. The Underveins and fungal city continue their quiet existence, and much of the ancient transit network remains unknown.

This overview covers the confirmed backbone of Chapter 1; a session-by-session chronicle and individual faction endorsements are still being verified.
