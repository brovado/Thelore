Chapter 1 — Canon Summary

The official summary of events, systems awakened, and unresolved threads from the first arc.

1. Scope of the Chapter

Covers the party’s actions through Sessions 1–18, culminating in the accidental restoration of the Forge and the immediate systemic effects that followed.

2. Initial Conditions

  • The Forge was inactive and unusable at the start of the chapter.
  • The Transit Destination Board in the connected control annex was unpowered.
  • No faction held control over the Forge.
  • The Underveins and the fungal city were quietly active and remained ignored.
  • Meraxis was known only to the party and one or two NPCs.

3. Major Locations Involved

  • The Forge.
  • The control annex/terminal station connected by massive power conduits, containing the Transit Destination Board.
  • The Underveins.
  • The fungal city.

4. Key NPCs and Factions

  • The Coalescence Society (placed a protective barrier; authority unresolved).
  • Meraxis (knowledge restricted to the party and one or two NPCs).
  • Vardros Thornveil (absent by the end of the chapter with no lingering presence).
  • The avatar manifestation (an unintended consequence of Forge activation).

5. Sequence of Major Events

  • The party followed a random map with no intent to restore the Forge.
  • The Forge was restored as a byproduct of that pursuit and became usable, though imperfect.
  • The Forge’s activation powered the Transit Destination Board in the control annex.
  • The avatar manifestation appeared as an unintended consequence of the activation.
  • The Coalescence Society established a protective barrier around the Forge, without resolving authority.
  • Vardros Thornveil departed entirely, leaving no lingering presence.

6. Systems Awakened or Altered

  • The Forge: restored to a usable but imperfect state.
  • The Transit Destination Board: powered on, displays names and sigils of unrecognized locations, and remains read-only.
  • Protective barrier: established by the Coalescence Society around the Forge site.

7. Consequences at Chapter End

  • The party is regarded as local heroes and useful but dangerous operatives.
  • The Forge remains operational but uncontrolled by any faction.
  • The Transit Destination Board remains active and unreadable beyond its displayed names and sigils.
  • The Coalescence Society’s barrier stands, while authority over the Forge remains unresolved.
  • Meraxis remains known only to the party and one or two NPCs.
  • Vardros Thornveil is gone with no lingering presence.
  • The Underveins and the fungal city remain quietly active and ignored.

8. Unresolved Threads

  • The Transit Destination Board’s destinations and sigils remain unidentified and unengaged.
  • Authority over the Forge remains unsettled despite the protective barrier.
  • The avatar manifestation exists as a consequence of activation without a defined role.
  • Meraxis remains a limited, closely held knowledge thread.
  • The Underveins and fungal city continue without engagement or resolution.
