Thel’aranor Gate — Transit Room

The safe staging chamber beneath Saint Lore’s public signage, where the Western Line is observed and chosen.

Environment

Massive power lines hum overhead, threading into ancient infrastructure that predates the city. Modern Saint Lore signage and access rails have been bolted onto the original stonework, marking a clean divide between civic control and the dormant machinery beneath.

What Happens Here

This room allows inspection, briefing, and selection. Players can review the Transit Board, consult with contacts, and inspect destination readouts without committing to travel. No commitment occurs inside the Transit Room.

Duration Tiers

  • 1-session: quick deployments or reconnaissance.
  • 2–3 sessions: standard missions with layered objectives.
  • 4–6 sessions: extended commitments with complex return conditions.

Commitment Rule

Once transit is exited at a destination, the party is committed. Return may be restricted until the local situation is resolved, and some destinations may change state after completion.
