Chapter II — Destination Index

Destination Index

How Chapter 2 destinations are structured, selected, and resolved.

Mission Codes

Each destination is labeled with a mission code (SL-###) used for briefings, logs, and return tracking. The code references Saint Lore staging records and does not imply ranking.

Duration Tiers

  • 1-session: safe fillers or rapid deployments.
  • 2–3 sessions: standard assignments with layered objectives.
  • 4–6 sessions: multi-session commitments with complex outcomes.

Lock-In Mechanics

Destinations are chosen from the Transit Room and entered via the Western Line. Once a destination is exited from transit, return may be restricted until resolution. This applies even to short missions.

Completion and Return States

Some missions conclude cleanly and allow immediate return, while others end in altered states. Completion may shift access rules, update board status, or leave lingering changes at the destination.

What to Expect

Not every destination is a high-stakes commitment. Some are safe fillers for reconnaissance or supply objectives, while others are multi-session endeavors tied to faction interests on the Western Line.
