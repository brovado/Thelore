Chapter 2 Overview

Chapter 2 shifts the campaign into modular, destination-driven play. The party operates out of Saint Lore, using the Thel’aranor Gate as a staging point. Missions are selected from the Transit Board and range from short excursions to multi-session commitments. Consequences accumulate quietly across destinations, with no single mandatory path.

The Western Line is active, but still only partially understood or controlled. The Coalescent Society remains a neutral administrative body with limited local presence. The six Ascendant Houses apply pressure in the background, each interpreting the line’s activation through their own priorities.

What Makes Chapter 2 Different

  • Destinations are selected by the players rather than prescribed.
  • Mission durations vary from 1 session to 6 sessions.
  • Inspection and briefing happen in a safe Transit Room before commitment.
  • Leaving transit may lock the party into a destination until resolution.
  • Some destinations have altered return states after completion.
