Chapter 1 — Chronicle

A narrative record of the chapter’s turning points and consequences.

The City Above the Deep

The chapter opened with the Forge silent and the terminal annex dark, a presence in the city’s memory rather than its workings. No faction held the site, and the Coalescence Society’s authority remained unresolved. Beneath the surface, the Underveins and the fungal city continued their quiet rhythms, active yet largely ignored. Knowledge of Meraxis remained a narrow thread held by the party and one or two others.

Descent into What Was Buried

The party’s early course was not a campaign to claim or revive the Forge. They followed a random map, moving through the city’s depths with practical aims rather than grand designs. That path brought them into proximity with the dormant machinery, without any prior effort to alter its condition or seize control over it.

The Accidental Return of Ancient Systems

The Forge’s restoration came as a byproduct of that pursuit, an unintended result rather than a declared project. When it awoke, it did not return as it once was: it became usable, yet imperfect. Its activation rippled through adjoining systems, and the control annex flickered to life. The Transit Destination Board powered on in the connected terminal station, revealing names and sigils that no one present could recognize. It allowed only observation—read-only, offering no immediate path to action.

Consequences Without Ownership

The reactivation drew response rather than resolution. The Coalescence Society raised a protective barrier around the Forge, asserting caution without settling authority. The party’s role in these events reshaped how the city regarded them: local heroes in some eyes, useful but dangerous operatives in others. Meanwhile, the avatar manifestation appeared as an unintended consequence of the Forge’s activation, a new presence without defined purpose. Vardros Thornveil departed entirely, leaving no lingering presence to complicate the chapter’s close.

What the City Learned (and What It Did Not)

The world shifted, but it did not settle. The Forge now operated in an imperfect state, untethered to any single faction’s control. The Transit Destination Board remained lit, displaying unrecognized destinations that could not yet be reached or interpreted. The Underveins and the fungal city continued, still unengaged. Meraxis stayed a closely held secret, no broader in its reach than it had been at the beginning.

The chapter ended with the Forge awake and the Transit Destination Board illuminated—movement now possible in principle, but not yet understood in practice. The story closed its first arc with the city altered and alert, and the wider world left open to what the next steps might become.
