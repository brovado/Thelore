3. Reference Pantry

These are prompts, not finished encounters. Expand them as the campaign grows.

1–6 · Travel Details

1Terrain & route
2Wildlife & natural signs
3Tracks, trails & signs
4Objects, ruins & discoveries
5People, activity & civilization
6Local lore, oddities & strange details

7 · Non-Combat Encounter

PeopleTraveler, merchant, hunter, pilgrim, messenger, patrol
ProblemsLost, injured, delayed, searching, fleeing, asking for help
OpportunitiesInformation, trade, warning, request, invitation, rumor
OdditiesStranger, unusual behavior, unexplained event, local custom

8–11 · Weather & Conditions

8Minor / inconvenient
9Noticeable / changes the feel of travel
10Significant / affects plans or choices
11Severe / becomes a major feature of the day
Pull from: rain, fog, wind, heat, cold, storms, snow, visibility, terrain conditions, unusual atmospheric events.

12 · Combat

ThreatMonster, beast, bandits, raiders, patrol, undead, faction
ReasonHunting, territory, ambush, pursuit, guarding, opportunity
TwistAnother party, environmental hazard, objective, mistaken identity
RememberThe 12 says combat is on the table; it does not dictate the encounter.

Multiple Results

1 result

Background influence. Use it if useful.

2 results

Strong influence. Consider making the category matter to the day.

3 results

Dominant feature. This category probably deserves attention.

4+ results

Exceptional. Consider combining them into one major circumstance rather than running several separate events.

The Dice Suggest. The DM Decides.
Multiple results can become one thing. A pair of weather rolls might be one storm. A detail roll and a combat roll might become tracks leading into an ambush. The system provides ingredients; Matthew cooks.
