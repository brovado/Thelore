Planet Information

General Description

Thelore is a vibrant world rich in magic and diverse cultures, where a lots of races live, with humans being the predominant species. The geography resembles Earth, with slightly more water space between regions like Saudi Arabia and Iraq, and the climate mirrors Earth’s weather patterns.

Civilizations

The Empire of Xandria

Leader: High Inquisitor Marcus Valen

Capital: Dodbrigh

Religion: Worship of Xandues, the God of Humanity

Ideology: Human Repopulation as a Divine Mandate

Culture: The Xandrian Empire believes that repopulating the world with humans is the best way to protect the material plane. They are highly organized, with a strong military presence and a theocratic government. Their cities are known for their grand temples, strict laws, and rapid expansion efforts. Non-humans are often viewed with suspicion or as second-class citizens, though some are tolerated if they contribute to the Empires's goals.

Geopolitical Influence: Controls 60% of the world's cities and land. Their influence is spreading rapidly as they seek to bring the entire world under the banner of Xandues.

The Cartel Confederacy of Sumaq

Leader: Don Ignacio Valverde

Capital: Vr'naar

Government: Cartel-run Oligarchy

Culture: The Valverde Cartel controls a vast network of trade routes, black markets, and resources. Their government is an oligarchy, with power concentrated in the hands of a few powerful families, particularly the Valverde family. Their cities are known for their vibrant culture, wealth disparity, and hidden networks. Loyalty and family ties are paramount, and betrayal is met with swift and brutal retribution.

Geopolitical Influence: Controls key trade routes and resources, with influence extending beyond their borders through black-market activities. They maintain a delicate balance of power with the Xandrian Dominion, often working in the shadows to undermine them.

The Harmonious Republic of Zhaolingy

Leader: Chancellor Mei Lian

Capital: Cleolesbu

Government: Confederacy of City-States

Culture: The Shēnglóng Confederacy is a collection of city-states that value community, innovation, and diversity. They are open to multiple races and cultures, leading to a highly populated and cosmopolitan society. Their cities are technological marvels, with advanced infrastructure and social systems designed to manage their large population. However, overpopulation is a constant challenge, leading to social tensions and resource scarcity.

Geopolitical Influence: While their land is limited, their influence is significant due to their technological advancements and economic power. They are a major trade partner and cultural hub, attracting people from all over the world.

The Celestial Sultanate of Iqar

Leader: Maharaja Arjun Suryavata

Capital: Brampound

Government: Theocratic Monarchy

Culture: The Sultanate of Iqar is a land rich in tradition, spirituality, and commerce. The society is structured around a blend of theocratic and monarchical rule, with the Maharaja and religious leaders holding significant power. Their culture is deeply tied to their religion, with elaborate rituals, festivals, and a strong emphasis on trade and craftsmanship. The empire is known for its skilled artisans, traders, and spiritual leaders who guide the people.

Geopolitical Influence: The Sultanate of Iqar controls several key trade routes and is known for its wealth in gold, spices, and textiles. Their spiritual influence extends beyond their borders, and they often act as mediators in conflicts between other factions.

History and Lore

Creation Myth

Thelore was birthed from the essence of titan elementals, which infused the world with magic known as Arcana. The gods harnessed this arcana to create life, landscapes, and the laws governing the world. However, conflicts arose, leading to the establishment of planes and a balance of power to maintain order.

Historical Events

  • The creation of the planes to manage the overpopulation of the material plane.
  • The ongoing conflict between gods that has shaped the political landscape of Thelore.

Magic and Technology

  • Magic System: High magic
  • Technology Level: moderatly advanced , with remnants of advanced civilizations from previous eras.

Flora and Fauna

  • Unique Creatures: Thelore hosts a variety of unique creatures, including those trapped from other planes due to the coalessence phenomenon.
  • Plant Life: Flora follows the established D&D lore, with magical plants and herbs that players can discover.

Economy and Trade

  • Resources: To be decided.
  • Trade and Commerce: The land trade is expedited by a lightning rail named Silk rail goes from far east at Cleolesbu to Dodbrigh and Brampound. The Air and sea trade is largely operated by elemental powered water and air boats.

Religion and Beliefs

Deities and Pantheons

Thelore has three major generations of gods, with most worship focused on the second and third generations.

Religious Conflicts

Followers of Xandues, the god of humanity, are on a conquest to populate the world, currently controlling about 60% of it.

Political Structure

  • Governments: Thelore is faction-based, with three tiers of governance including global factions, large government factions, and local guilds.
  • Laws and Justice: Each region has its own legal systems, influenced by the culture and governance of the major factions.

Deity Descriptions

Mylin

the Goddess of Nature, is almost plain-looking, with fair skin, shoulder length, light brown hair, and eyes the color of rusted metal. She is tall and muscular, and tends to look sad. She usually wears simple robes dyed dark violet and dark aqua. She is also associated with duels, mountains and cities. She is often worshipped by lawbreakers. Her few followers can be primarily distinguished by the charms they carry with them. There are widespread temples to her at crossroads. There are many myths involving her duels with Vilyn. She is the sister of Laendar. She is known to give aid to mortal villians.

Paiz

the Goddess of Prophecy, Fire and Archery, is shockingly beautiful, with brown skin, short, light brown hair, and eyes the color of summer sky. She is very tall and slightly plump, and has a faraway expression. She usually wears light and airy robes dyed brown and blue-black. She is also associated with storms, business and poetry. She is often worshipped by commoners. Her many followers can be primarily distinguished by the clothing they wear. There are quite a few shrines to her in mountains. There are many myths involving her tumultuous affairs with Ilotor. She is the mother of Seayrlo. She is known to sometimes hinder mortal heroes.

Vilyn

the Goddess of the Sea and the Sun, is unusually beautiful, with tanned skin, waist length, red-gold hair, and eyes the color of fallen leaves. She is surprisingly short and willowy, and usually has a guarded expression. She usually wears little clothing, dyed grey-brown. She is also associated with shadows, the undead and dance. She is often worshipped by warriors. Her surprisingly numerous followers can be primarily distinguished by their daily prayers. There are a large number of sacrifices to her in mountains. There are many myths involving her ongoing arguements with Mylln. She is the sister of Mylln. She is known to give advice to great mortal heroes.

Gindol

the God of Earth, Jesters and Passion, is divinely beautiful, with tanned skin, long, curly pale golden hair, and eyes the color of polished oak. He is medium height and solidly built, and almost never smiles. He usually wears formal attire dyed pale orange. He is also associated with assassins and youth. He is often worshipped by lawbreakers. His many followers can be primarily distinguished by the charms they carry with them. There are a few carvings of him in large cities. There are many myths involving his ongoing arguements with certain mortals. He is the brother of Oryth. He is known to have relationships with mortal warriors.

Oryth

the Goddess of death, is eerily beautiful, with copper skin, shoulder length, straight black hair, and eyes the color of jade. She is tall and somewhat muscular, and tends to look sad. She usually wears scant clothing, dyed grey. She is also associated with politics and birds. Her many followers can be primarily distinguished by their jewelry. There are a small number of shrines to her near rivers. There are many myths involving her rough relationship with Gindol. She is the sister of Or'nel. She is known to appear to dying mortals.

Dack

Dack, the God of Dreams, Fear and Crossroads, is divinely beautiful, with fair skin, long, rippling bright red hair, and eyes the color of lightning bolts. He is of average height and broad-shouldered, and is always smiling. He usually wears a cloak dyed pale blue. He is also associated with poetry and charisma. He is often worshipped by clerics. His few, but dedicated followers can be primarily distinguished by the holidays they observe. There are many temples to him in cities. There are many myths involving his wild romance with certain mortals. He is the cousin of X'klyan. He is known to give advice to mortals seeking forgiveness.

Freamalra

Freamalra, the Goddess of Light and Luck, is an exotic beauty, with fair skin, waist-length, wavy light brown hair, and eyes the color of polished oak. She is of average height and muscular, and is usually laughing. She usually wears worn, ragged clothing dyed grey. She is also associated with rivers, armor, and revelry. She is often worshipped by nobles. Her large numbers of followers can be primarily distinguished by their hairstyle. There are many shrines to her near lakes. There are many myths involving her tumultuous affairs with certain mortals. She is the daughter of Seayrlo. She is known to appear to mortal warriors.

Arull

Arull, the God of Destruction, Sacrifice and Spring, is surprisingly plain, with copper skin, short, flowing pale red hair, and eyes the color of rusted metal. He is tall and slightly plump, and seldom smiles. He usually wears armor and a helmet dyed brown. He is also associated with science and magic. He is often worshipped by the poor. His surprisingly numerous followers can be primarily distinguished by the holidays they observe. There are numerous altars dedicated to him in forests. There are many myths involving his ongoing arguments with certain mortals. He is the brother of Seayrlo. He is known to help virtuous mortals.

Seayrlo

Seayrlo, the God of Cats, is an exotic beauty, with ivory skin, short, flowing white hair, and eyes the color of the green sea. He is tall and of hearty build, and rarely laughs. He usually wears a cloak dyed pale yellow and dark aqua. He is also associated with courtesans and autumn. He is often worshipped by clerics. His numerous followers can be primarily distinguished by their hairstyle. There are a small number of sacrifices to him in most villages. There are many myths involving his scandalous relationship with Cyvin. He is the father of Freamalra. He is known to sometimes place obstacles in the way of mortal heroes.

Cyvin

Cyvin, the Goddess of Opposites, Vengeance and Good, is quite beautiful, with ivory skin, short, russet hair, and eyes the color of forest moss. She is very tall and slightly plump, and sometimes seems very sad. She usually wears scant clothing, dyed white and dark blue. She is also associated with psychology and messengers. She is often worshipped by commoners. Her large numbers of followers can be primarily distinguished by the holidays they observe. There are many statues of her at crossroads. Chondath

the God of Time, is quite beautiful, with ashen skin, shoulder length, rippling white hair, and eyes the color of gleaming steel. The Arcane Consortium

Main location: Jugor's Rest Unaffiliated Parent location: Feling Xandria

Ideology

The Arcane Consortium values knowledge, innovation, and the responsible use of magic to advance society.

Goals

  • Expand magical knowledge and understanding
  • Develop new magical technologies and applications
  • Preserve and study ancient elven magical artifacts and lore

The Consortium often collaborates with the Flame Seekers on artistic magical projects but sometimes conflicts with the Verdant Enclave over resource use.

The Radiant Order

The Radiant Order often clashes with the Shadow Syndicate but maintains cordial relations with most other factions.

The Shadow Syndicate

Main location: Jugor's Rest Unaffiliated Parent location: Rauglyl Sumaq

Ideology

The Shadow Syndicate believes in personal freedom, the power of information, and the necessity of a flexible moral code to achieve one's goals. They prioritize self-interest and the accumulation of wealth and influence over traditional notions of morality or civic duty.

Goals

  • Maximize personal and collective wealth for Syndicate members
  • Control the flow of information and valuable goods in Jugor's Rest
  • Maintain a balance of power that allows for profitable operations
  • Expand influence through strategic alliances and calculated risks
  • The Syndicate often finds itself at odds with the Radiant Order's strict regulations but maintains a complex relationship of mutual benefit with other factions.
  • Many merchants are drawn to the Syndicate for its promise of wealth and influence, seeing it as a path to success in a competitive market.
  • While not above using underhanded tactics, the Syndicate generally avoids unnecessary violence or chaos, preferring to maintain a stable environment for their operations.
  • The Syndicate is known for its extensive network of contacts and its ability to get things done quickly and discreetly, making it a valuable, if controversial, ally to many in Jugor's Rest

The Enclave often collaborates with the Radiant Order on public health initiatives but frequently disagrees with the Arcane Consortium over the use of natural resources for magical purposes.

Jugol's Rest

Located in: Central Wildlands Main Trade: Pilgrimage and Cooffee Established: Ancient Era, exact date unknown

Location

Nestled in the rugged wilderness near the east coast of Africa, Jugol's Rest is an ancient and sacred site surrounded by deserts and rainforests. This remote area is difficult to reach, making the journey to Jugol's Rest a significant pilgrimage for those seeking spiritual enlightenment and connection to the ancient world.

Overview

Jugol's Rest is a revered pilgrimage destination, known for its deep spiritual significance and ancient history. The site is believed to be the final resting place of the first elves, who established a powerful connection to the primal forces of nature here. Pilgrims come from all over to experience the sacred energies of the place, participate in ancient rituals, and seek prophetic visions.

The Sacred Coffee: Elvenbrew

  • Elvenbrew: Jugol's Rest is home to a unique species of coffee plant known as Elvenbrew, cultivated by the ancient elves. This coffee is renowned for its mystical properties, believed to enhance spiritual awareness and open the mind to prophetic visions.
  • Ceremonial Use: The brewing and drinking of Elvenbrew is a sacred ritual at Jugol's Rest. Pilgrims partake in this ceremony, often at dawn, as a way to connect with the spiritual energies of the site and seek clarity for their journeys.
  • Cultural Importance: Elvenbrew is not only a symbol of the pilgrimage but also a valuable commodity. Pilgrims often take small amounts back home as a token of their journey.

The Hyenas: Sacred Guardians

  • Sacred Role: The hyenas of Jugol's Rest are more than just animals; they are considered sacred guardians, believed to be the incarnations of ancient spirits who protect the site. These creatures are deeply respected by the locals and pilgrims alike.
  • The Feast of the Guardians: Every evening, the people of Jugol's Rest offer food to the hyenas in a ritual known as the Feast of the Guardians. Pilgrims participate in this ritual, which is seen as a way to earn the favor of the ancient spirits and protect the site from harm.
  • Hyena Oracles: Some druids and shamans at Jugol's Rest can interpret the behavior of the hyenas to gain insights or prophecies. This practice is respected and often sought after by those looking for guidance.

Rituals and Festivals

  • Journey of the Elders: Many wood elves undertake a final pilgrimage to Jugol's Rest in their later years, believing that passing away in this sacred place will allow their spirits to join the ancient elves in their eternal vigil.
  • Festival of Rebirth: Held annually during the spring equinox, this festival celebrates the cycles of life, death, and renewal. It includes rituals like planting sacred trees and performing ceremonial dances.
  • The Coalescence Vigil: During the Coalescence event, pilgrims and members of the Coalescence Society gather at Jugol's Rest to hold a vigil. They participate in rituals designed to strengthen the beacon that protects the area from planar incursions.

The Coalescence Society

  • Beacon of Stability: Jugol's Rest remains safe during the Coalescence event thanks to a special beacon erected by the Coalescence Society. This beacon mimics the presence of a population, stabilizing the area and preventing dangerous planar entities from crossing over.
  • Society's Role: The Coalescence Society plays a crucial role in maintaining the safety and sanctity of Jugol's Rest, especially during the annual Coalescence.

Visiting Jugol's Rest

Pilgrims who visit Jugol's Rest often find themselves immersed in the ancient traditions and rituals of the site. The journey itself is challenging, but the rewards are great: a deep connection to the spiritual energies of the ancient elves, a chance to partake in sacred ceremonies, and the opportunity to gain wisdom through visions and prophecies. Whether seeking enlightenment, renewal, or protection, Jugol's Rest offers a unique and powerful experience to all who make the journey.

Marks

Dragon marks

Dragonmarks are physical manifestations of the Draconic Prophecy. They have appeared on five of Thelore's common races. They are more intricate than a birthmark and more distinct than a tattoo. There are twelve families of dragonmarks, known as the dragonmarked houses. Originally there were thirteen, but no living creature on Thelore possesses the destroyed Mark of Death of House Jual. In addition to being a status symbol in Thelore, the dragonmarks provide the bearer with magical talents.

Aberrant dragonmarks

Aberrant dragonmarks can be clearly distinguished from a standard house dragonmark and no two are alike. The dragonmarks are passed through bloodlines, and when two members of different dragonmarked families reproduce, it often results in what are called 'aberrant dragonmarks'. The aberrant marks do not manifest the same way each time, so it is possible for two individuals with completely different-looking marks to produce the same magical effect using the mark. Indeed, each aberrant dragonmark is completely unique to its bearer. Originally, those with aberrant marks were seen as outcasts and outsiders. Often they were shunned by the houses and eked out a pitiful existence. Many members bearing these aberrant dragonmarks have since united in their differences and formed House Tarkanan, most notably present in Sharn.

Manifestations

Living Dragonmark
A cleric uses this dragonmark to its full potential. Dragonmarks typically appear in the bearer's adolescence as they pass through puberty, but this is not exclusively the case. There are three distinct manifestations of each dragonmark that correlate in size, intricacy, and power. As the dragonmark gains power (from least to greater), it will expand in size and complexity rather than forming a different mark. Often, the mark will manifest first as the least mark, and through study and focus, the bearer increases their power, and with this increase, the dragonmark also increases, granting additional abilities. However, there are some who will manifest a lesser mark or even greater mark without explanation. House members who manifest even the least dragonmark, however, can expect at least some change in their lifestyle as they find new and better employment opportunities within their house guilds as well as new social avenues to explore.

Heir of Siberys

Heir
Rurik d'Kundarak, a dwarf fighter with the Siberys Mark of Warding.
Much rarer are the so-called Heirs of Siberys who possess the Siberys mark, which covers much, if not all, of their body. Siberys marks are named after the progenitor dragon Siberys. A dragonmarked individual might labor all their life and never manifest the Siberys Mark, while an unmarked member of a house bloodline might suddenly manifest the mark overnight. It is unpredictable and unexplained, despite steady research by top minds across Khorvaire. The Siberys mark grants the bearer extraordinary power based upon which mark they manifest. These Siberys dragonmarks can lead otherwise unknown or undiscovered members of a house to suddenly rise in importance within their own ranks, and those who were formerly unmarked may have a hard time adjusting to this new fame.

Jugor's Rest is an ancient city with a size far exceeding its current population needs. Due to the influx of various factions and its rich historical backdrop, several districts have emerged, each with unique characteristics, some underutilized, offering both opportunity and decay.

The Northern Docks

Main location: Jugor's Rest

The Northern Docks are the primary port area for Jugor's Rest, handling much of the city's trade and transportation by water. It is also a lively area with various businesses and services for dock workers and travelers.
