Title:
**Chapter 2 — Opening Dossier**

1. Current World State
- Jugol’s Rest has entered a period of heightened scrutiny and political tension following the accidental restoration of the Forge.
- The city recognizes the party as local heroes while also treating them as capable, unpredictable operatives.
- Authority around the Forge remains unresolved, creating space for competing interpretations, cautious positioning, and stalled decisions.
- The Underveins and the fungal city continue their quiet activity beneath the surface, still largely ignored in official considerations.
- Knowledge of Meraxis remains limited to the party and one or two NPCs, preventing broader coordination or policy.

2. The Forge: Status and Limitations
- The Forge is functional but imperfect, operating without comprehensive understanding of its condition or full capabilities.
- Its restoration was accidental rather than the result of a deliberate program or controlled activation.
- No faction currently controls the Forge; access and authority are unsettled.
- The Coalescence Society’s barrier provides protection only and does not constitute governance or operational control.
- The system’s behavior remains partially opaque, with consequences that cannot yet be fully predicted.

3. The Transit Network: Observations
- The Transit Destination Board is located in the control annex/terminal station connected to the Forge.
- It powered on directly as a consequence of the Forge’s activation and remains active.
- The board displays names and sigils of unrecognized locations; the interface is read-only with no selectable destinations.
- The structure appears modular and purpose-built for a larger network, indicating scale beyond local use.
- Its design and endurance suggest considerable age, with components built to persist through long inactivity.

4. Known Unknowns
- What conditions are required for the Transit Destination Board to permit selection or activation.
- What the displayed names and sigils correspond to and whether any are reachable from Jugol’s Rest.
- Whether the Forge’s imperfect state can be stabilized or understood without unintended effects.
- What the avatar manifestation represents and how it relates to the Forge’s operation.
- How long the Coalescence Society’s barrier can remain effective without broader authority or support.
- What role, if any, the Underveins and fungal city play in the systems now active.

5. Risks of Activation
- Interaction with ancient systems may trigger outcomes not anticipated by current knowledge or safeguards.
- Political friction is likely if access to the Forge or the Transit Destination Board is perceived as exclusive or uncontrolled.
- Metaphysical consequences, including further manifestations, remain a credible risk given prior unintended outcomes.
- Logistical strain may follow any attempt to engage with systems that extend beyond the city’s planning or supply capacity.

6. Strategic Implications
- The prospect of movement beyond Jugol’s Rest shifts the campaign’s scope from local recovery to wider engagement.
- Opening or interpreting the transit network could reshape alliances, authority, and resource priorities within the city.
- Choosing not to engage is also a strategic posture, with consequences for influence, preparedness, and public perception.
- The absence of a controlling faction creates both opportunity and instability, requiring careful coordination.

7. Guidance Moving Forward
- Proceed with measured investigation that prioritizes safety, documentation, and repeatable observations.
- Maintain alliances that can share risk and validate findings, while recognizing unresolved authority.
- Treat the Forge and the Transit Destination Board as systems with unknown dependencies rather than tools under command.
- Avoid actions that presume control until evidence supports it, and preserve flexibility for multiple outcomes.

The world is now connected in ways it was not before. Movement is possible, but understanding lags behind capability. Chapter 2 begins not with action, but with choice.
